# FIKRA Visual Direction Brief

**Status:** Approved direction for design exploration; not final brand identity<br>
**Reference:** Previously reviewed 20-second creative-studio showcase video<br>
**Applies to:** Public marketing site and its relationship to the authenticated workspace

## 1. Creative objective

FIKRA should feel like a premium Arabic creative-production system: intelligent, visually confident, human-trusted, and capable of turning an idea into launch-ready work. The reference video sets the ambition for pacing, composition, and visual presence; it is not a template to copy.

## 2. Experience split

| Surface | Character | Primary job |
|---|---|---|
| Public marketing site | Cinematic, bold, high-contrast, expressive | Explain the promise and convert visitors into starting a project |
| Authenticated workspace | Calm, warm, precise, fast | Help returning users manage work, studios, Brand Memory, experts, tasks, and files |

The common thread is premium craft and clarity. The difference is intensity: marketing earns one expressive moment; work screens optimize repeated use.

## 3. Visual character

- Premium but approachable: Figma professionalism with Canva warmth.
- Arabic-led editorial composition with short, decisive headlines.
- Strong contrast, generous space, and controlled density.
- Large typographic moments balanced by clear body copy and obvious actions.
- Visual proof of transformation: idea → brief → options → expert refinement → delivery.
- Human expertise and production readiness are visible, not hidden behind generic AI language.

## 4. Homepage narrative

### 4.1 Cinematic Hero

Suggested promise: **حوّل فكرتك إلى علامة جاهزة للنمو**

- Primary CTA: **ابدأ مشروعك**
- Secondary CTA: **استكشف الاستوديوهات**
- Central visual concept: a controlled field of visual elements transforms between a logo, palette, package, website, and content system.
- The transformation suggests continuity through shared Brand Memory.
- Copy and CTAs appear immediately; the visual scene is decorative support.

### 4.2 What FIKRA is

A concise explanation: FIKRA is not a single design generator. It is an Arabic-first production system of AI guidance, specialized studios, shared brand memory, and human expert review.

### 4.3 Studios showcase

Present Brand, Logo, Menu, Packaging, Website, and other approved studios as connected production capabilities, not a generic card grid. Each studio should show a recognizable output or transformation.

### 4.4 Guided journey

1. Explain the idea in natural Arabic.
2. Review the structured brief.
3. Receive multiple directions.
4. Refine with AI and experts.
5. Approve and receive production-ready files.

### 4.5 Quality and trust

Show the commitments that make FIKRA credible:

- Arabic-market understanding.
- Shared Brand Memory.
- Human sign-off for launch-critical assets.
- Consistent production quality.
- Clear price and time expectations.
- Real deliverable formats, not preview-only images.

### 4.6 Intelligent agents

Adapt the reference video's team presentation into specialized FIKRA agents and expert roles. Each role must communicate a real responsibility, not an invented personality without product function.

### 4.7 Final CTA

End with one strong conversion action: **ابدأ مشروعك مع FIKRA**.

## 6. What not to copy

- The reference palette or black background as final identity.
- Compressed Latin typography that does not translate well to Arabic.
- Continuous motion across every section.
- Low-contrast supporting text.
- Pointer-dependent discovery or hidden navigation.
- Heavy effects on mobile.
- LTR ordering or compositions merely mirrored after design.
- Cinematic effects inside the authenticated workspace.

## 7. RTL and Arabic composition

- Compose in RTL from the first wireframe.
- Arabic headline wrapping must be art-directed at major breakpoints.
- Mixed Arabic/Latin names, file types, prices, and numbers require bidi testing.
- Directional sequencing begins at inline-start on the right.
- Any cursor-follow or horizontal interaction needs equivalent touch, keyboard, and static behavior.
- Final type choices wait for UI-04; the design may specify typographic roles without selecting a font package.

## 8. Motion direction

- One decorative Hero scene is allowed under UI-07's explicit exception.
- Routine interactions remain `100–250ms`.
- Reveal/celebration motion is reserved for first meaningful draft and final approval/delivery.
- Reduced-motion users receive the complete composition without animation.
- Mobile receives a simpler or static Hero.
- The page must remain understandable with all motion disabled.

## 9. Responsive behavior

- Mobile prioritizes the promise, primary CTA, and one clear visual proof.
- Studio exploration becomes swipe-safe or vertically stacked without hiding content behind gestures.
- Oversized statements scale and wrap intentionally; they never crop essential Arabic words.
- Desktop pointer effects are enhancements, never prerequisites.
- Marketing backgrounds may be full bleed while readable content follows UI-06.

## 10. Visual proof over decoration

Prefer real FIKRA outputs and coherent transformation sequences over abstract particles alone. The visual language should demonstrate what the product produces: identity systems, packaging, menus, websites, content, files, and expert-reviewed outcomes.

## 11. Pending decisions before final visual implementation

- Final FIKRA brand palette.
- Arabic and Latin typography system (UI-04).
- Icon system (UI-05).
- Final logo/wordmark behavior.
- Exact Hero visual production method and measured performance budget.
- Final list and priority order of public Studios.
- Approved proof points, pricing claims, and human-review language.

## 12. Acceptance criteria

- The result feels original to FIKRA, not like a reskin of the reference.
- A visitor understands the product promise and next action without interacting with the Hero scene.
- Marketing and workspace feel related but intentionally different in intensity.
- RTL, Arabic wrapping, mixed content, keyboard use, and reduced motion are verified.
- Mobile remains fast and visually complete with a static Hero.
- No unapproved final color, font, icon, or dark-mode decision is introduced.
