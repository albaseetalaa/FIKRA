# Fikra AI Launch Markets and Expansion Vision

Status: Approved
Decision date: 2026-07-29
Target release: v1

## Primary v1 Launch Markets

Fikra AI v1 will launch in:

- Saudi Arabia
- Jordan

Saudi Arabia and Jordan are Fikra AI's two primary v1 launch markets.

Both markets have equal strategic, product, operational, marketing, support, and commercial priority.

Neither market has precedence over the other.

Neither market will be treated as secondary, experimental, pilot-only, or delayed.

## Equal Product Experience

Customers in Saudi Arabia and Jordan will receive:

- The same approved v1 studios and capabilities
- The same product-quality standards
- The same AI execution and validation standards
- The same level of customer support
- The same release and improvement cadence
- Equal marketing and commercial attention

## Market Localization

Each project will include a market profile that determines:

- Target country
- Target city when applicable
- Currency
- Primary language
- Preferred content tone
- Audience and cultural context
- Local market terminology
- Pricing and billing configuration

Supported currencies for v1:

- Saudi Riyal (SAR)
- Jordanian Dinar (JOD)

## AI Market Awareness

Fikra AI agents must use the selected project market when generating:

- Business recommendations
- Brand positioning
- Marketing strategies
- Social-media content
- Website copy
- Menu and packaging guidance
- Customer personas
- Competitor and market analysis

Agents must not treat Saudi Arabia and Jordan as interchangeable markets.

Outputs should reflect the selected country's audience, culture, terminology, commercial context, and customer expectations.

## Future Arab Market Expansion

After establishing the product in Saudi Arabia and Jordan, Fikra AI will expand across the Arab world.

Each Arab market will receive appropriate localization for:

- Language and dialect preferences
- Currency
- Cultural context
- Local commercial practices
- Customer expectations
- Market terminology
- Pricing and payment requirements

The Arab-market expansion must preserve Fikra AI's core advantage in understanding Arabic businesses, customers, brands, and content.

## Global Expansion Vision

After expanding across the Arab world, Fikra AI will enter international markets.

Global expansion will include:

- Multiple interface languages
- Multilingual AI outputs
- Country-specific market profiles
- Multiple currencies
- International billing and payment support
- Localized legal and compliance requirements
- Region-aware brand and marketing guidance

## Architecture Rule

The core platform must remain country-independent and must not hard-code Saudi Arabian or Jordanian assumptions.

Countries, currencies, languages, market rules, and localization preferences should be represented through configurable market profiles wherever practical.

This allows Fikra AI to expand into new Arab and global markets without rebuilding the core product.

## Launch Rule

No approved v1 capability may be intentionally released in one primary launch market while being withheld from the other, unless a documented legal, payment-provider, or infrastructure restriction makes equal availability temporarily impossible.
