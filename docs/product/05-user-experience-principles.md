# Fikra AI User Experience Principles

Status: Approved
Decision date: 2026-07-30
Target release: v1

## Product Experience Goal

Fikra AI is not a collection of isolated AI generators.

It is an intelligent operating workspace that helps a customer understand, build, launch, and grow a business or brand through coordinated agents, structured decisions, shared project memory, and guided next actions.

The customer remains the final decision-maker.

## Primary v1 Users

Fikra AI v1 is designed primarily for:

- Founders developing a new business idea
- Owners of small and medium businesses
- Restaurants, cafes, stores, service businesses, and emerging brands
- Marketers and designers managing work for clients
- Small teams collaborating on one or more projects

The interface and onboarding should adapt to the customer's situation rather than forcing every customer through the same process.

## Entry Paths

New customers should choose one of three primary starting paths:

### New Idea

For customers who are still exploring or defining a business idea.

Fikra AI should help them clarify:

- The business concept
- The customer problem
- The proposed solution
- The target market
- The target audience
- The business opportunity
- The initial brand direction

### Existing Business

For customers who already operate a business.

Fikra AI should help them:

- Import or describe their existing business
- Upload current brand materials
- Identify missing or inconsistent information
- Evaluate current brand and marketing needs
- Preserve existing approved decisions
- Recommend the highest-impact improvements

### Client Work

For marketers, designers, consultants, and agencies managing projects for other businesses.

Fikra AI should support:

- Separate client projects
- Independent project memory
- Version history
- Review and approval workflows
- Controlled collaboration
- Clear ownership and access boundaries

## Progressive Onboarding

Onboarding should feel like a guided conversation rather than a long form.

Fikra AI should collect information progressively, including:

- Business idea or current business
- Industry
- Country and city
- Target audience
- Customer problem
- Proposed value
- Brand personality
- Competitors
- Budget
- Objectives
- Existing assets

Customers may skip questions and return later.

Fikra AI should explain why important information is requested and how it will improve the resulting work.

## Project Lifecycle

Each project moves through five connected stages:

### Discover

Understand the idea, business, audience, market, opportunity, and constraints.

### Decide

Review and approve strategic, commercial, and brand decisions.

### Build

Create the brand identity, content, packaging, menu, website, and other approved assets.

### Launch

Prepare the project for market entry, including launch content, website readiness, campaign materials, and final approvals.

### Grow

Support recurring content, campaigns, brand consistency, updates, and improvement after launch.

The lifecycle should guide the customer without preventing direct access to approved studios.

## Project as the Core Unit

Each business or client engagement is represented as an independent Project.

A project may contain:

- Project Profile
- Market Profile
- Brand Memory
- AI Conversations
- Studios
- Artifacts
- Versions
- Sources
- Decisions
- Approvals
- Comments
- Usage History
- Activity History

Project information must not automatically mix with another project.

## Project Status and Next Best Actions

The project home should clearly show:

- Current lifecycle stage
- Overall completion
- Completed decisions
- Missing information
- Items awaiting review
- Current risks or inconsistencies
- Recent activity
- Fikra Credit usage
- Recommended next actions

Fikra AI should normally recommend no more than three high-value next actions at one time.

Each recommended action should explain:

- Why it matters
- What input is required
- What output will be produced
- Which agents or studios will be involved
- Estimated Fikra Credit usage when material

## Information and Decision Types

Fikra AI must distinguish between:

### Customer Input

Information directly provided or confirmed by the customer.

### Imported Source

Information extracted from a customer file, link, or connected source.

### AI Proposal

A recommendation or draft generated by Fikra AI that has not been approved.

### Approved Decision

A decision explicitly approved by an authorized customer or team member.

### Derived Output

An artifact created from approved information and decisions.

### Superseded Decision

A previously approved decision replaced by a newer approved decision.

### Archived Item

An item retained for history but no longer active.

AI proposals must not silently become approved project facts.

## Artifact Status

Important artifacts should support lifecycle states such as:

- Draft
- Needs Review
- Approved
- Superseded
- Archived

Creating a new version must not destroy earlier versions.

## Shared Decision Memory

Approved project decisions should become structured project memory that approved studios and agents can reuse.

Examples include:

- Target audience
- Market
- Value proposition
- Brand personality
- Tone of voice
- Visual direction
- Colors
- Typography
- Product positioning
- Customer promises
- Approved terminology

This allows Social Content Studio, Website Studio, Packaging Studio, Menu Studio, AI Chat, and future studios to work from the same confirmed foundation.

Agents must not silently override confirmed decisions.

When a proposed output conflicts with an approved decision, Fikra AI should:

- Identify the conflict
- Explain the affected decision
- Ask whether the customer wants to preserve or replace it
- Record the result through an explicit approval

## Role-Based Experience

Fikra AI should adapt the experience according to the user's role.

### Owner

Focuses on:

- Direction
- Approvals
- Progress
- Budget
- Team access
- Final decisions

### Admin

Manages:

- Members
- Project settings
- Access
- Operational configuration

### Editor

Creates and modifies:

- Content
- Brand assets
- Project information
- Draft decisions

### Viewer

Can review approved project information and artifacts without editing.

### External Reviewer

A future or limited collaboration role that may allow a client or specialist to:

- View selected artifacts
- Comment
- Approve
- Request changes

Exact role availability and subscription limits will be finalized in the PRD.

## Collaboration Principles

Collaboration should preserve:

- Project ownership
- Permission boundaries
- Decision accountability
- Activity history
- Version history
- Approval history

High-impact changes should record who approved them and when.

## Industry Templates

Fikra AI should not require every customer to begin from an empty workspace.

Initial industry templates may include:

- Restaurant or cafe
- E-commerce store
- Service business
- Personal brand
- Food product
- Technology company
- Salon or clinic
- Real-estate project

A template may configure:

- Relevant onboarding questions
- Recommended agents
- Expected outputs
- Industry terminology
- Suggested lifecycle order
- Market examples
- Common risks
- Relevant studios

Templates must not lock the project into assumptions that the customer cannot change.

The initial v1 template list will be finalized in the PRD based on market priority and implementation effort.

## Learning From Customer Actions

Fikra AI may record product-improvement signals such as:

- Whether an output was accepted
- Whether it was edited
- Whether it was rejected
- Whether it was regenerated
- Which version was approved
- Which workflow was completed or abandoned
- Which steps caused confusion or delay

These signals must follow the approved data, memory, and trust principles.

Aggregated and de-identified operational signals may improve the product.

Use of identifiable or project-specific content for broader training or evaluation requires the approved contribution controls.

## Arabic-First Experience

Arabic must be a first-class product experience rather than a secondary translation.

Fikra AI should support:

- Complete right-to-left interface behavior
- High-quality Arabic interface copy
- Arabic and English project outputs
- Independent interface and output languages
- Formal Arabic and appropriate local tone
- Saudi and Jordanian market awareness
- Correct handling of Arabic typography
- Correct handling of mixed Arabic and English text
- Correct handling of numbers, currencies, dates, and punctuation
- Brand tone options such as formal, friendly, youthful, premium, local, or professional

A customer may use the Arabic interface while generating English outputs, or use the English interface while generating Arabic outputs.

## Consistency Across Studios

All studios should use the same approved project context.

Fikra AI should prevent avoidable inconsistencies such as:

- Website copy using a different audience from the marketing strategy
- Social content using a different tone from the approved brand voice
- Packaging using outdated colors
- Menu content conflicting with approved positioning
- AI Chat recommending a direction that supersedes approved decisions without warning

## Continuous Value After Launch

Fikra AI must continue providing value after the initial brand or launch work is completed.

The Grow stage may include:

- Monthly priorities
- Content calendars
- Upcoming campaigns
- Relevant market occasions
- Brand consistency checks
- Assets requiring updates
- Pending reviews
- Suggested experiments
- Project improvement recommendations

Social Content Studio is a central recurring-use capability because it helps customers return weekly or monthly rather than using Fikra AI only once.

## Experience Simplicity

The product should use progressive disclosure.

Customers should see the information and controls required for their current task without being overwhelmed by every available feature.

Advanced settings should remain accessible without dominating the primary workflow.

Fikra AI should:

- Use clear language
- Explain unfamiliar terms
- Avoid unnecessary technical jargon
- Preserve customer progress
- Prevent accidental destructive actions
- Show expected AI usage before expensive operations
- Provide clear recovery paths after failures

## Product Experience Principle

Fikra AI should always help the customer answer:

- Where is my project now?
- What has already been decided?
- What still needs my approval?
- What is the best next action?
- Which outputs are based on approved information?
- What changed and who changed it?
- How can I continue growing the project?

The customer should experience Fikra AI as an organized digital team and project operating system, not as an unstructured AI chat interface.
