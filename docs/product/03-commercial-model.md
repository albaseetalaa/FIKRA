# Fikra AI Commercial Model

Status: Approved
Decision date: 2026-07-29
Target release: v1

## Subscription Model

Fikra AI will use monthly and annual subscriptions.

The initial subscription structure will include:

- Limited free trial
- Starter
- Pro
- Business

Final prices, included usage amounts, and detailed feature limits will be determined after cost modeling and market validation.

Saudi Arabia and Jordan will receive the same product capabilities and quality standards.

Pricing may be localized independently for Saudi Riyals and Jordanian Dinars based on each market's commercial conditions. Localized pricing must not make either market a lower-quality product experience.

## Fikra Credits

Each paid subscription will include a defined monthly usage allowance called:

- Fikra Credits
- رصيد فكرة

The customer-facing product should not expose raw AI-token terminology.

Fikra Credits represent access to resource-intensive AI operations while keeping usage understandable and predictable for customers.

## Operations That May Consume Credits

Credits may be consumed by operations such as:

- Running AI agents
- Generating or regenerating content
- Generating images and logo concepts
- Market and competitor analysis
- Coordinating multiple agents on one task
- Producing large campaigns or content calendars
- Other operations with meaningful AI or infrastructure cost

The exact credit cost of each operation will be defined after measuring real provider and infrastructure costs.

## Operations That Do Not Consume Credits

Normal workspace actions should not consume credits, including:

- Opening projects
- Viewing saved results
- Manually editing text
- Organizing project files
- Reviewing previous versions
- Downloading and exporting completed work
- Managing project settings

## Usage Notifications

Customers will receive clear usage notifications at:

- 70 percent: early notification
- 90 percent: important notification
- 100 percent: monthly credits exhausted

Usage information must be visible before the customer starts an operation expected to consume a significant amount of credits.

## When Monthly Credits Are Exhausted

When the included monthly credits are exhausted, the customer's account and saved work remain accessible.

The customer may continue to:

- Open projects
- View previous work
- Edit work manually
- Download and export work
- Manage project information

Only new resource-intensive AI operations are paused.

The customer may then:

- Purchase additional Fikra Credits as a one-time purchase
- Upgrade the subscription immediately with fair prorated billing
- Wait for credits to renew at the next billing cycle

## Billing Protection

Fikra AI v1 will not automatically charge a payment method for additional credits.

Every additional-credit purchase or subscription upgrade requires explicit customer approval.

Optional automatic credit replenishment may be considered later for eligible Business customers.

## Revisions and Version History

Manual editing of saved outputs will not consume credits.

AI regeneration may consume credits because it creates a new AI operation.

Creating a new version must not delete or overwrite the previous version.

Customers should be able to:

- Review earlier versions
- Compare versions where practical
- Restore an earlier version
- Continue editing from a selected version

## Expert Hub

Expert Hub is deferred beyond v1 and is currently planned for v1.5.

It may later allow customers to request paid human review or professional assistance from roles such as:

- Brand designers
- Marketing specialists
- Content specialists
- Business experts

Human review will be an optional paid service and will not be required to use the core Fikra AI product.

## Commercial Principle

The commercial model must balance:

- Predictable customer pricing
- Transparent usage
- Sustainable AI operating costs
- Natural subscription upgrades
- Protection from unexpected charges
- Continued access to customer-owned work
