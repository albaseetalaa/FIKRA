# Fikra AI Product Scope

Status: Approved
Decision date: 2026-07-29
Target release: v1

## Product Direction

Fikra AI v1 will focus on the Brand & Marketing Studio.

The objective is to help founders and businesses transform an idea into a consistent brand, marketing assets, and recurring social-media content through coordinated AI agents and a persistent project workspace.

## Approved v1 Scope

- AI Chat
- Workspace and Brand Memory
- Logo Studio
- Brand Studio
- Social Content Studio
- Menu Studio
- Packaging Studio
- Basic Website Studio

## Social Content Studio

The v1 Social Content Studio will support:

- Content strategy based on the project's identity and objectives
- Weekly and monthly content calendars
- Post, Story, and Reel ideas
- Arabic and English captions
- Headlines and calls to action
- Short-form video and Reel scripts
- Creative briefs for individual content items
- Brand-aware tone, colors, and identity guidance
- Repurposing one idea for multiple social platforms
- Review and export before publishing

The following capabilities are deferred beyond v1:

- Direct publishing and scheduling to social platforms
- Complete AI video production inside Fikra
- Comment and direct-message management
- Influencer campaign management
- Paid advertising campaign management
- Advanced analytics and platform-performance integrations

## Deferred Product Studios

The following studios are not part of v1:

- Architecture Studio
- Interior Studio

They will be reconsidered after the first release produces sufficient evidence about customer demand, operational complexity, and AI execution costs.

## Scope Rule

New capabilities must not be added to v1 unless they directly strengthen the approved Brand & Marketing Studio workflow and are formally reviewed as product-scope decisions.
